Accessibility Statement

Access for people with disabilities

The Destiny is committed to providing a Site that is usable by as many people as reasonably possible, including people who use keyboards, screen readers, magnification, voice input, captions, or other assistive technologies.

Standard and status

The ongoing goal is alignment with the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.2 Level AA. This is a goal, not a claim that every page has been independently audited or fully conforms. Accessibility is reviewed through a combination of automated checks, keyboard review, semantic markup, responsive design, contrast review, and user feedback.

Measures

  • Use semantic headings, landmarks, labels, link text, and alternative text where applicable.
  • Support keyboard navigation, visible focus, browser zoom, text resizing, reduced-motion preferences, and responsive layouts.
  • Avoid relying on color alone to communicate required information.
  • Maintain an accessibility feedback channel and prioritize barriers that prevent access to essential information or services.

Known limitations

Older documents, third-party embeds, maps, booking tools, payment flows, social-media content, user-submitted media, and legacy pages may not provide the same level of accessibility. The operator may not control third-party code, but will try to provide an accessible alternative for essential information or service when requested.

Feedback and accommodation

If you encounter a barrier, email people@talkcircles.io with the page address, a description of the issue, and the assistive technology or browser used if you are comfortable sharing it. Do not include sensitive health or financial information. The operator will review accessibility reports as promptly as reasonably practicable and will try to provide a reasonable alternative while investigating an essential-access barrier.
